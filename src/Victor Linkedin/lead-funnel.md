# Lead Generation Funnel & UX Architecture

## 1. Funnel Stages (The "Quickstart" Pathway)
1. **Traffic**: Arrives from LinkedIn Profile link or direct CTA on portfolio. ("How dependent is your business on spreadsheets, manual work and people's memory? Take the 10-minute Business Systems Health Assessment.")
2. **Landing View**: Brief context setting. No email capture yet.
3. **Assessment Engine**: 25-40 quick questions, progressively disclosed.
4. **Immediate Hook**: Displays the Overall Score (e.g., 58/100) and the biggest weakness immediately to prove value.
5. **Email Gate**: "Your detailed diagnostic is ready." Collects Name, Email, Company.
6. **Results Dashboard**:
   - Score & 3 Bottlenecks
   - Evidence & Consequences
   - Opportunity areas
   - Smallest next step (The "what and why", not the detailed "how")
7. **Monetization CTA**: Dynamic recommendation for one of four **Operational Quickstarts**.

## 2. The Operational Quickstarts (Entry Offers)
The assessment engine dynamically routes the user to the most relevant fixed-scope intervention:

1. **Workflow Automation Quickstart**: Identify one high-friction workflow and automate the repetitive parts. (For: repetitive manual processes).
2. **Data Visibility Quickstart**: Turn one messy operational data process into a reliable source of truth. (For: fragmented spreadsheets/data).
3. **Lead Follow-Up Quickstart**: Connect lead capture, tracking and follow-up into one reliable workflow. (For: leads falling through the cracks).
4. **Internal Tool Quickstart**: Build one focused internal tool around a specific operational bottleneck. (For: businesses forcing unique workflows into unsuitable SaaS).

*Pricing for all Quickstarts is positioned as "Starting from $X" subject to a qualification step, rather than fixed at $1,500.*

## 3. The Strategic Hierarchy
1. **Free**: Business Systems Health Assessment (Generate qualified demand)
2. **Entry**: Quickstart (Get paid + prove capability)
3. **Core**: Systems implementation (Main project revenue)
4. **Expansion**: Custom software / automation (Larger engagements)
5. **Recurring**: Fractional Ops / systems maintenance (Recurring revenue)
6. **Premium**: Systems Architecture Audit (High-value diagnostic, earned later)

## 4. Lead Qualification Step
When a user clicks the Quickstart CTA, they are taken to a short qualification application (Company, Role, Team size, Current process, Problem, Desired outcome, Budget range, Timeline) to ensure fit before a consultation.
